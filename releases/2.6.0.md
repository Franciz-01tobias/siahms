## What this release is about

Two things most people will notice. The **inbox** gets a filter: show only the tickets assigned to you, hide closed ones, or both, whichever way the folders are grouped, with every folder count following along. Grouped by analyst, your own folder now sits at the top as **My tickets**, so on a large desk you are not scrolling past everybody else to find yourself.

The other is for installs that look after **more than one company**. Assets can now be moved between companies, adding an asset asks which company it is for, and a location such as a shared data centre can be used by every company. The *All companies* view now shows every company's people and tasks, not just the last one you picked.

Most of this came from people running FreeITSM: the inbox filter from [#149](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/149), and the company work from a list of requests sent in by somebody running FreeITSM for several companies.

## New features

- **[Just my tickets, or no closed ones](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/wiki/Inbox-Filter-and-My-Tickets).** Asked for in [#149](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/149) by An Duong. A **filter** button beside Search offers **Show: All / Mine** and **Hide closed**. It narrows the folders and the list together in any grouping, and every folder count follows it. While a filter is on, the button shows a dot and a label sits beside the list title, so nobody wonders where half their tickets went. Your choice is saved for you alone. "Closed" means every status you have marked as closed, not only one called Closed.

- **My tickets at the top, when grouped by analyst.** Your own analyst folder moves to the top under that name, with a line separating it from everyone else. It still opens to a count per status, and dragging a ticket onto it assigns it to you.

- **Search keeps finding closed tickets.** The search window has its own **Include closed tickets** switch, on by default and independent of the inbox filter, because a search is usually a hunt for an old ticket. It covers searching inside tickets too.

- **[Move an asset to another company](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/wiki/Moving-an-Asset-Between-Companies).** An asset's **Key info** shows its **Company**; change it and the asset moves. Anything that exists only in the old company - its location, or a type or status that company added for itself - is cleared, and the message says what. The move is refused, with the reason, if the new company already has an asset with the same name or asset tag, or if the asset is held by a person from another company. Invisible on a single-company install.

- **Adding an asset asks which company it is for.** Whenever you can reach more than one company, **Add asset** starts with a Company question, and the type, status and location lists follow your answer. In the *All companies* view there is no default, so a new asset can no longer land in whichever company you happened to pick last.

- **[Locations shared across companies](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/wiki/Shared-Asset-Locations).** Under **Assets → Settings → Locations**, a location can be marked **Shared with every company** - a data centre or head office that several clients' equipment sits in. Every company can pick it alongside its own, and can add its own rooms inside it. Only someone with access to every company can share, change or delete one, and it cannot be unshared while other companies' assets are still there.

## Improvements

- **The Assets help has a new section, "Assets and companies"**, covering moving assets, what is cleared and refused, the company question, and shared locations.

- **The folder pane's wiki page now covers grouping by Team**, which it had not mentioned since teams arrived.

- **This release's new screens are English-only in the other thirteen languages for now.** All thirteen complete languages still cover everything up to 2.5.0; the 35 strings added here fall back to English cleanly and will be translated in the next batch.

## Fixes

Everything here was broken in 2.5.0.

- **In Team view, dragging a ticket onto a team did nothing.** The folder lit up as a drop target and then nothing happened, and dropping onto a status under a team did the same. It now gives the ticket to that team, leaving the assigned analyst alone.

- **In Team view, dropping a ticket on Unassigned cleared its department** and left its team as it was. It now clears the team, which is what Unassigned means in that view.

- **In Team view, clicking a status under a team did nothing.**

- **The *All companies* view showed only the last company you picked** in **Tickets → Users** and on the **Tasks board**, rather than every company you can see.

- **The bottom of the inbox was cut off.** Every pane ran slightly below the window, so on a long folder list the last folder - usually Trash - could not be scrolled into view.

- **A bright white bar in dark mode** on **Assets → Settings** (Asset types, Asset statuses) and in the same place in **Tickets → Settings**, where a company's own entries are separated from the shared ones.

## Upgrading

1. Pull the new code, or rebuild the Docker image.
2. Sign in as an administrator and run **System → Database Verification**. It adds one column, for shared locations.
3. Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl-F5).

Nothing else to do by hand. Until Database Verification has run, everything works except sharing a location, which simply is not offered.

## Rollback

Safe - this release only adds to the database, so you can go back to 2.5.0 with the code alone.
