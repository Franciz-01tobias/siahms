## What this release is about

**A submitted form is now a record you can keep.** Export one as a PDF carrying your own logo, the answers and who approved it - one at a time, or tick several and take them as separate files or a single bundle. It was asked for to keep an audit trail, and that is what it produces: real searchable text, not a picture of the screen.

Built on top of that, **collections** group the submissions of several forms under one name - *Staff Survey 2026*, an audit round, an onboarding wave - with one page showing everything filed under it. When the exercise is over you close the collection, and **you** decide what closing means for your organisation.

**The mobile rollout finishes Tickets and Checklists.** The rota, dashboard, calendar, users, triage, CSAT and all five guides now work properly on a phone, as does Checklists & SOPs. Nothing on the desktop changed.

And one for anyone on an isolated network: **FreeITSM no longer loads any JavaScript or CSS from the internet.** If "Save to PDF" did nothing on your server, this is why, and it is fixed.

Upgrading asks nothing of you beyond running Database Verification.

Rollback: safe - this release only adds to the database.

## New features

- **[Keep a submitted form as a PDF](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/wiki/Submission-PDF-Export-Developer-Guide).** Open a submission and choose **Export as PDF**, or use the download icon on its row without opening it at all. The document carries your logo, the submission number, who submitted it and when, the approval decision and any comment, and every answer - including answers to questions you have since removed from the form. Files are named the way you would file them yourself, *Form title - Person - date*, using your own date format.

- **Several submissions at once.** Tick the rows you want and choose **Bundle** for one PDF with a page per submission, or **Separate** for a file each. The tick in the table heading takes everything currently shown.

- **[Collections](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/wiki/Form-Collections-Developer-Guide).** Create a collection in **Forms → Settings → Collections**, then put forms in it - from the collection itself, or from the folder icon on any form in the list. It is entirely optional; most forms, like a laptop request, belong to none. Several forms can share one collection.

- **One page for everything in a collection.** Open a collection to read every submission filed under it, across all of its forms, with a date filter and the same single, bundle and separate exports. Because a collection's forms ask different questions, the list shows what they have in common and the answers appear when you open a row.

- **A collection can be closed, and you choose what that means.** Three options: closing is simply a label saying the exercise is finished; or its forms stop accepting new submissions; or they also disappear from the self-service portal. Whichever you choose, closing never changes the forms themselves - so reopening a collection restores exactly what was there, including a form you had deliberately kept off the portal.

- **A submission remembers where it was filed.** Move a form to a different collection later, or take it out of collections altogether, and last year's responses still read as last year's. Nothing you do to a form afterwards rewrites what its earlier submissions belonged to.

## Improvements

- **[Tickets works on a phone, all of it](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/wiki/Mobile-Friendly-Tickets).** The rota gives you three ways to read it - week, day, or one analyst - and you pick; the dashboard, widget library, users, calendar, triage, mailbox activity, CSAT and all five guides now behave on a small screen. Thirteen pages in total.

- **[Checklists & SOPs works on a phone](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/wiki/Mobile-Friendly-Checklists)**, which completes the [mobile rollout](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/wiki/Mobile-Friendly) for the modules people use daily. Editing a template puts Back and Save in a sticky footer, and the scope filters move into a full-screen sheet instead of taking the top half of the list.

- **Forms can be linked to a collection from either end** - per form on the forms list, or as a tick list on the collection. A form belongs to one collection, so ticking one that is already in another moves it; the picker tells you where each form currently lives before you tick, and names what moved when you save.

- **The submissions page uses the full width of the screen**, and the forms list shows a form's status, portal and collection as pills under its title rather than squeezed into a narrow column.

## Fixes

- **"Save to PDF" did nothing on a server with no outbound internet.** Morning checks, the asset table and Knowledge fetched their PDF library from a public CDN at the moment you clicked, so on an isolated network the button simply did not respond and nothing said why. Every third-party library now ships inside the application - see [Offline and air-gapped installs](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/wiki/Offline-and-Air-Gapped-Installs).

- **A submitted form was showing you less than FreeITSM had recorded.** The approval decision, who made it and any comment were stored but never displayed on the submission panel.

- **A workflow error could fail a submission that had already been saved.** If an automation attached to a form ran into certain kinds of error, the submission was written to the database but reported as a failure - so people filled the form in again.

- **The rota's day and analyst views had no way to change the day or the analyst** on a phone, tapping a user in Tickets → Users appeared to do nothing, and the CSAT comment was squeezed into a column one word wide.

- **Dark mode fixes** on the new selection bar, and the collections screen now matches the other settings pages.

## Upgrading

1. Pull the new code, or rebuild the Docker image.
2. Sign in as an administrator and run **System → Database Verification**. Collections need a new table and two columns; until you run it, the Collections tab tells you so and the rest of Forms is unaffected.
3. Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl-F5).

Collections are switched off in the sense that nothing exists until you create one, and no existing form or submission is changed by upgrading.
