## What this release is about

Somebody I had never met built a whole module for FreeITSM and offered it to the project. **Checklists & SOPs** is the work of [Santhosh Srinivasan](https://github.com/srinivasansanthosh), and it is the first time this has happened. That is why this is 2.0.0 rather than 1.10.0 - the number is a thank-you, not a warning. **Nothing in this release asks anything of you**, and rollback is safe.

Alongside it: **six languages** moved substantially forward, five of them essentially complete. The **staff rota** gained a much faster way to fill itself in - copy a shift and paste it into a block of cells, a whole day, one person's week, or a whole week at a time. And an error that three people reported after upgrading, which was never their database, is gone for good.

## New features

- **Checklists & SOPs, a new module.** Write a procedure once - onboarding, offboarding, a firewall change, a VPN fault - and attach it to any ticket. Steps can be marked **mandatory**, can ask the analyst for a value as they tick them off (an asset tag, a serial number), and can suggest which role normally does them. Keywords let FreeITSM offer the right procedure on a ticket before anybody goes looking for it, and every tick is recorded in the ticket history with who and when. Under **Tickets → Settings → Checklists** you decide what happens when somebody closes a ticket with mandatory steps outstanding: **warn** and record an internal note naming the steps and who closed it, or **block** the close until they are done. Whichever you pick applies everywhere a ticket can be closed, including bulk actions, the REST API and workflow automation. Try it with **System → Demo data**, which seeds five realistic procedures.

- **Fill the rota in a fraction of the time.** Copying a single shift already worked; now the paste can land on far more than one cell. Drag across a block of cells, or hold Ctrl and pick them out, then right-click and paste into the lot. Hover a **day heading** and the whole column lights up; hover an **analyst's name** and their whole week does - right-click either to paste into all of it. Where some of those cells already have a shift, the menu offers both ways by name: *Paste into all 7*, or *Paste into 6 empty cells*. You can also **copy a whole day, or one person's whole week**, and paste it onto another, and **clear** a day, a person or a selection the same way. A plain click still opens the shift editor exactly as before.

- **Close a ticket with a one-off message.** Put `[ticket_closed_message]` anywhere in an active **Ticket closed** email template, and from then on closing a ticket asks the analyst for a sentence - *"the spare charger is on your desk"*, *"closing as agreed on the phone"* - which lands exactly where you put the placeholder. Nothing is stored and there is nothing to configure beyond the template. Type nothing and the line simply is not there. The prompt only appears if the placeholder is actually in your template, so desks that do not want it never gain a click. Asked for by [mbsouth](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/142).

- **System → Contributors.** Thank-you cards for the people who have made a substantial contribution, in the product rather than buried in a commit log.

## Improvements

- **Six languages moved forward.** **Polish** went from 37% to complete - by far the largest jump - and **Spanish** from 81%. **Danish**, **German** and **Hindi** were finished off, and **Gujarati** went from 34% to 72%. Together that is over sixteen thousand strings. Two faults surfaced on the way that no coverage score can see: a German screen that had been rendering in English for months because it was calling a key that no longer existed, and a label on the service status page that no language could translate correctly because it was assembled from fragments in English word order.

  One caveat, so you are not surprised: the newest screens in *this* release - the Checklists module and the rota's new menus - are still English in every language. They fall back cleanly and nothing is broken; they are the top of the next translation pass.

- **The merge codes for email templates are all in one list.** Fifteen of them existed; two were buried in a sentence after the list, so a reader genuinely could not find them. They are now split into *always available* and *only on some triggers*, with the trigger named.

- **Help that matches what the product does.** The Checklists module arrived without a help page - it has a full guide now, reachable from its own Help button. The rota's help had never mentioned copy and paste at all, not even the version that shipped in 1.9.0. Branding help now covers the three sign-in screens it configures, and System → Contributors has a page of its own.

## Fixes

- **"The index list is out of date" after upgrading is gone.** Three people reported this over three releases ([#121](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/121), following [#113](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/113)) - a red error on **Database Verification** telling you to run a script and commit two files. **There was never anything wrong with your database.** Two files that both ship with FreeITSM, and that both describe the schema, disagreed with each other. Fixed, and a check now runs on every change so it cannot ship again. If it ever does reappear, it says plainly that your data is fine and asks you to report it, instead of showing you a red error you cannot act on.

- **A background image uploaded on the Self-Service Portal or Home tab went to the analyst login screen** ([#137](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/137)), replacing whatever was there while the portal showed nothing. Each tab now keeps its own. Fixed by Santhosh Srinivasan.

- **Completion times on a checklist were recorded an hour ahead**, and deleting a template left its steps behind in the database.

- **The merge-code help under the email template editor showed its own formatting as text**, so readers saw a literal `<strong>` rather than a bold word. Spotted by a reader, which is the only reason anybody looked.

## Upgrading

1. Pull the new code, or rebuild your container.
2. Sign in as an administrator and run **System → Database Verification**. This is not optional - this release adds six tables for the Checklists module.
3. Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl-F5). This release updates cached JavaScript, and a stale file looks like a broken feature.

Rollback: safe - this release only adds to the database.

## Anything to do by hand

Nothing. The Checklists module appears in the waffle menu for anyone whose role allows it, and everything else is on by default or off until you configure it.
