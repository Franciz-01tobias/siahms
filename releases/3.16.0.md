## What this release is about

FreeITSM now talks to the systems around it. Zabbix problems open incidents by themselves and resolve them when they clear. 3CX shows who is calling before you answer and notes every call on the caller's ticket. Teams channels can be told about urgent tickets, SLA breaches and each team's new work. The first three were contributed by Carl Steynvaard of Open Resilience, who built and ran them in production before offering them to the project - thank you, Carl. Domains also gains subdomains and certificates you record by hand, and stops reporting SPF and DMARC as missing when it simply could not look them up.

## New features

- **Zabbix alerts become incidents.** System → Integrations → Zabbix: download the import file, import it into Zabbix, and a monitoring problem opens an incident at the priority you map its severity to, linked to the server in the CMDB and filed in that server's company. Repeats go on the same ticket, and the recovery resolves it. **Send a test alert** shows you a real incident without waiting for a real problem. Details: [Zabbix](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/wiki/Zabbix-Integration).
- **3CX: see who is calling, and keep every call.** System → Integrations → 3CX gives you a template to upload into 3CX. While the phone rings, 3CX shows the caller's name and opens their ticket; when the call ends, it is noted on that ticket by the analyst who took it, or opens a ticket if they have none. Which ticket a call goes on is your choice, and two people sharing a number are never guessed between. **Try a call** shows what a call would do without writing anything. Details: [3CX](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/wiki/3CX-Integration).
- **Click to call.** Set a dial address (the 3CX web client, or `tel:` for any softphone) and phone numbers on people, supplier contacts, Tickets → Users and a ticket's From line get a **Call** button.
- **Teams channel alerts.** System → Integrations → Microsoft Teams: paste a channel's Workflows address and tick what it should hear about - new tickets at or above a priority, tickets raised to it, the tickets for chosen teams, SLA warnings and breaches, status page incidents, approvals waiting. Each arrives as a card with an **Open in FreeITSM** button. Every alert is an ordinary workflow, with its history and retries. Details: [Microsoft Teams alerts](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/wiki/Microsoft-Teams-Alerts).
- **A Teams card for any workflow.** A new **Microsoft Teams (Workflows card)** message format for the Send webhook action, for the Workflows addresses that replaced Teams' old connectors in May 2026. New merge codes `{{link}}`, `{{ticket.url}}` and `{{app.url}}` put a link to the ticket in any message.
- **Subdomains in Domains.** Add students.example.org and it is linked to example.org. Its registration and renewal are the parent's, while its own email, DNS and certificates are checked - including its parent's DMARC, which now applies instead of a false "No DMARC record". Details: [Subdomains](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/wiki/Domains#subdomains).
- **Recorded certificates.** Certificates the checks cannot reach - an internal server, a VPN appliance, a SAML signing certificate - can be recorded on a domain with their type, provider and expiry, and get the same alerts, calendar entries and Watchtower count as the checked ones. The certificate types are a list you can edit under Domains → Settings. Details: [Recorded certificates](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/wiki/Domains#recorded-certificates).

## Improvements

- **Workflows can act on a new ticket's team.** "A ticket is created" now carries the team the ticket arrived with, so a workflow can be limited to, say, the L2 team's new tickets.

## Fixes

- **Domains reported SPF and DMARC as missing when it could not look them up.** If the server could not reach DNS - for example a network that blocks DNS-over-HTTPS - every domain showed "No SPF record" and "No DMARC record" and lost points for them. A failed lookup is now reported as one, with the reason, and no grade is given until the checks can run; on Windows the Automatic resolver also tries two other ways before giving up. Details: [A failed DNS lookup was reported as a missing record](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/wiki/Domain-Checks-Failed-Lookup-Read-As-Missing).
- **Integration help pages** loaded two of their files from the wrong place, so the notification bell and some phone layout were missing on them.

## Upgrading

1. Pull the new code, or rebuild the Docker image.
2. Sign in as an administrator and run **System → Database Verification**. It creates the tables for Zabbix, 3CX, Teams alerts and recorded certificates, and links any subdomains already in your Domains register to their parents.
3. Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl-F5). This release updates cached scripts.

Things to know:

- The integrations do nothing until you set them up under **System → Integrations**.
- **Domains → Settings → Certificate types** is a new settings tab with its own permission. Administrators have it; grant it to other roles in **System → Roles** if they maintain Domains settings.
- The new screens are in English for now; translations follow.

## Rollback

Safe. This release only adds to the database, so you can go back to 3.15.1.
