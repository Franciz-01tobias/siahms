## What this release is about

A small release with one new diagnostic. Someone reported that their ticket folder numbers all showed 0, apart from their own "My tickets" folder. That pattern almost always comes from how teams are set up, not from a fault, but it was hard to see why from the screen alone. The new Debug Tool shows exactly where the tickets go missing and what to change. Nothing else changes.

## New features

- **Find out why the ticket folder numbers say 0.** **System → Debug Tools → D018** takes the analyst who sees the wrong numbers and follows every ticket through each filter behind the folder list - the Trash, merged and snoozed tickets, the company in use, and the departments their teams can see - showing how many each step removes. It also lists every team with its members and departments, departments no team covers, and analysts in no team, and ends with a plain-English verdict and the fix. The usual cause: an analyst in a team that is not linked to any department gets folder numbers that count only the tickets assigned to them, so "My tickets" has a number while everything else says 0. Link the team to its departments in **System → Teams**, or take the analyst out of the team. *(Corrected after release: this was a fault in how the numbers were counted, fixed in 3.7.0 - a team with no departments now counts every ticket with no department. The ticket list itself was never hidden.)*

## Upgrading

1. Pull the new code, or rebuild the Docker image.
2. Sign in as an administrator and run **System → Database Verification**.
3. Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl-F5).

## Rollback

Safe - nothing was changed in the database, so you can go back to 3.6.0 with the code alone.
