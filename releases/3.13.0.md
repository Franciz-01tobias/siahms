## What this release is about

This release is about **finding your way around**. Every module that creates something now puts **New** in the same place - the right of a strip just under the module's menu - instead of a dozen different spots, so you stop hunting for it when you switch modules. The optional **sidebar** can show module names, and **preference templates** let you give a whole team the same timezone, language, sidebar and the rest in one go - including every new analyst from their first sign-in. On the portal, requesters can now **see the answers they gave** when they raised a ticket. Run Database Verification after upgrading.

## New features

- **New is always in the same place.** Every module that creates something - Tickets, Assets, Software, CMDB, Domains, Contracts, Changes, Problems, Tasks, Knowledge, Checklists, Process Mapper, Forms, LMS, Workflow, Network Mapper, Calendar, Projects, Service Status and War Room - now has **New** at the right of a strip just under the module's menu, on every view of the same records: a module's Table, Calendar or Timeline view gets it too. On a phone it stays on screen instead of hiding inside a Filters or Tags panel. Tasks gains a general **New** for the first time - it asks for a title and opens the task - and the **+** on each board column stays as a shortcut. Things that are not "make a record" keep their own words: **Plan** for maintenance, **New message** in War Room, **Assign** and **Upload** in LMS. See the [UI style guide](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/wiki/UI-Style-Guide). Suggested in [discussion #185](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/discussions/185).
- **Preference templates.** **System → Preference Templates** saves a named set of preferences - say "Service desk" - taken from somebody's real setup, usually your own: set up your Preferences the way you want a team to start, press **New**, and tick which settings go in (timezone, language, date format, money, notifications, the sidebar, module order and more). **Apply** gives it to the people you choose, in one go; it replaces their choices for those settings only, and they can change anything afterwards. Mark one template **for every new analyst** and anyone created from then on - on the Analysts screen, through the API, LDAP or single sign-on - starts with it. See [Preference templates](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/wiki/Preference-Templates). Suggested in [discussion #124](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/discussions/124).
- **Requesters see the answers they gave.** On the portal's **My Tickets**, a ticket now shows **Your details** - the answers to the extra fields the requester was asked when they raised it, as they are now, so a correction your team makes shows up. **Tickets → Settings → Custom fields → Show requesters the answers they gave** puts them under the ticket's title (the default), in a panel beside the conversation, or nowhere. Fields only your team fills in are never shown. Suggested in [discussion #193](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/discussions/193).

## Improvements

- **The sidebar can show module names.** **Preferences → Display → Sidebar → Module names**: icons only (as before), open with names when you point at it - over the page, so nothing moves - or always open with names. A **Pin** button at the bottom of the sidebar switches between the last two. See [The sidebar](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/wiki/App-Sidebar).
- **Team folders can show only your own teams.** **Tickets → Settings → General → Team folders**: every team (as before) or only the teams each analyst belongs to. On an install with more than one company, the **Owner** list on a ticket and the **Assign** menus now offer only people who can work in that ticket's company. Requested in [#176](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/176).
- **Afrikaans, Dutch and Italian are complete** - every screen, including the fourteen modules that had never been translated into them. The screens and settings new in this release are in English in every other language for now, and will be translated in a later release.

## Fixes

- **A long form inside a task was cut off**, hiding its later questions and the Submit button. Contributed by [@Dr4inder](https://github.com/Dr4inder) ([PR #198](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/pull/198)).
- **Taking a course in LMS**, the lesson used only a narrow column, and the Back and Next buttons at the bottom were partly off the screen.
- **On Morning Checks, the sidebar's icons were squashed** and its settings button had disappeared.
- **The people search box on Assets → Users was squeezed to a sliver.**
- **Some translations still described screens as they were before 3.12.0**, after the English wording had changed. Those strings now show the current English until they are translated again.

## Upgrading

1. Pull the new code, or rebuild the Docker image.
2. Sign in as an administrator and run **System → Database Verification** - it adds the table for preference templates.
3. Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl-F5) - this release updates cached scripts and styles on almost every page.

## Rollback

Safe - nothing was removed from the database, so you can go back to 3.12.0.
