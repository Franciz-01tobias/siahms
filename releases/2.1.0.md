## What this release is about

**Calendar sync is no longer Microsoft-only.** Scheduled tickets and tasks can now go into a calendar on **Nextcloud, Baïkal, Radicale, iCloud, Fastmail** or any other CalDAV server, as real two-way appointments - and you can run as many calendar connections as you need, Microsoft 365 and CalDAV together, with each analyst choosing theirs. That finishes the last piece of what [mbsouth](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/133) asked for in #133, and the address-book side of that request grew too: customers can now correct their own contact details from the portal, with the change sent to your address book if you allow it, and analysts can add somebody to an address book without leaving FreeITSM.

Separately, **Tickets can now insist on certain fields being filled in before a ticket closes**, and warn, email someone, or refuse outright when they are not.

**If you use a CardDAV address book, please upgrade.** Since 1.8.0, adding one failed on Save, and the people it imported could never sign in to the self-service portal. Both are fixed.

Nothing in this release asks anything of you beyond running Database Verification, and rollback is safe.

## New features

- **Mandatory fields before a ticket closes.** **Tickets → Settings → Mandatory fields** lists thirteen ticket properties - priority, type, category, resolution code, owner and more - to tick as required at closure. Then choose what happens when somebody closes a ticket with one empty: **warn**, **warn and email** the people you name, or **refuse** the close; a warned close can also be recorded on the ticket. The analyst is asked before the close, whichever way they close it - the status dropdown, the right-click menu, dragging it onto a closed status, or a bulk action - and workflow automation obeys the same rule.

- **Calendar sync with CalDAV servers.** Under **System → Calendar sync**, add a CalDAV server by its address. Each analyst then signs in with their own calendar account under **Preferences → My work calendar**, presses **Find** and picks a calendar - an app password is the better choice where the server offers one. Appointments work both ways: move one in your calendar app and the ticket follows on the next scheduled check. A reminder or note you add to one of these appointments stays put when the ticket changes. I built and tested it against Baïkal; it follows the standard rather than anything Baïkal-specific.

- **As many calendar connections as you like.** Microsoft 365 for most people and a Nextcloud for a few, several CalDAV servers, or more than one Microsoft tenant. Each connection is added, tested and removed on its own, and when there is more than one, each analyst picks theirs under **Which calendar**. Changing or removing a connection asks first and only affects the people on it.

- **System → Portal profile.** Decide which of their own contact details people may change under **My Account** in the portal - job title, office, phone and mobile (all four until you change it). Department, employee ID, manager and email are never offered, and the page says why. A second setting, **off by default**, lets customers who came from a CardDAV address book with write-back switched on correct theirs too, with the change sent to the address book first and saved only once it is accepted - the self-correction data-protection rules ask for, without anyone retyping it.

- **Add to address book.** Somebody who emailed in exists in FreeITSM but not in your address book. Switch on **Let analysts add people to this address book** on that address book, and they get an **Add to address book** button in **Tickets → Users** and **Assets → Users**. It asks first, creates their contact, and links the person you already have rather than making a copy. It refuses if the address book already has their email address, and if the address book only imports one group or tag, the new contact goes into it - with a final check that the next import will see them, and the contact taken back out if not.

- **See where each person comes from, and filter by it.** Both user screens now name the directory, address book or sign-in provider a person's details come from, and a new **Source** filter lists only the people from one of them, or only those added in FreeITSM, with a count beside each.

## Improvements

- **System → Calendar sync uses the full width of the screen.**
- **Locked details in My Account now look locked.** A detail your directory owns was drawn exactly like one you could type in, most noticeably in the dark theme.
- **Signing out of the portal asks in FreeITSM's own dialog** rather than the browser's.
- **Help that matches the product.** The new screens have help pages, and several existing ones were corrected along the way - the Assets user list, for one, still claimed to show only people holding equipment. Preferences help now covers **My work calendar**.

## Fixes

- **Adding a CardDAV address book failed on Save** ([#133](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/133)), on every version since 1.8.0, although **Test** worked. Fixing it turned up a quieter problem: three screens save these settings and none of them shows all of them, and saving from one could reset what it does not show - renaming an address book cleared its chosen book and switched write-back off, renaming an LDAP directory switched its sync off, and the settings pages moved a source back to no company. Settings a screen does not send are now kept.
- **People imported from an address book could never sign in to the portal**, whatever password they were given - they were told to use single sign-on. An address book is where their details come from, not a way to sign in. Present since 1.8.0.
- **A workflow could close a ticket that the checklist rule should have stopped** (new in 2.0.0). Workflows now obey both the checklist and mandatory-field rules, and a refused close shows the reason in the workflow's run history.
- **The Checklists settings tab misbehaved for anyone who was not a full administrator** (new in 2.0.0): a role with Checklists but not Statuses saw an empty tab, and one without Categories got a Save button that did nothing.
- **One bad address on an SLA escalation list stopped the alert reaching everyone listed after it.** Every recipient is now tried.
- **A signed-out analyst opening a System page got a blank page** instead of the sign-in screen, and a non-administrator got the same instead of being sent home.
- **My Account in the portal closed if you selected text in a field and let go of the mouse outside the dialog**, losing whatever you had typed.
- **Assets → Users told you to edit an address-book contact "in the directory"** even when write-back was on and nothing was locked.

## Upgrading

1. Pull the new code, or rebuild your container.
2. Sign in as an administrator and run **System → Database Verification**. This release adds one setting column for address books.
3. Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl-F5).

If you add a CalDAV connection, changes made in a calendar come back through the same scheduled job Microsoft 365 uses, `cron/calendar_sync_pull.php` - every five minutes is sensible. CalDAV servers do not send change notifications, so there is no faster option.

Rollback: safe - this release only adds to the database.

## Anything to do by hand

Nothing. Every new setting is off until you switch it on, apart from Portal profile, which starts with exactly the four details the portal already offered.

One caveat, so you are not surprised: the newest screens in this release - Mandatory fields, Portal profile, the calendar connections and the address-book options - are in English in every language for now. They fall back cleanly and nothing is broken; they are at the top of the next translation pass.
